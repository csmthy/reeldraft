How it works

Five ways to judge a film.

{{ index_name }} turns every major rating system into five pillars and one {{ score_name }}. The same numbers power the {{ site }} daily game. The pool holds {{ '{:,}'.format(n_films) }} films, and {{ '{:,}'.format(n_complete) }} have all five pillars, so they're ranked and draftable.

Commercial

Worldwide gross in constant 2025 dollars, using the BLS CPI. Star Wars counts as a $4B film, not a $775M one.

Critics

Rotten Tomatoes critics score and Metacritic, averaged when both exist.

Audience

IMDb user rating.

Cinephile

Letterboxd weighted average rating.

Awards

Oscar nominations plus wins. A win counts double.

The numbers

Each pillar is a percentile rank across the whole pool, from 0 to 100. A 99 beats 99% of ranked films on that pillar. Box office is inflation-adjusted first, so older films compete fairly on money. The {{ score_name }} is the average of the five pillars, and only films with all five are ranked.

Where a pillar has two sources (Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic), this version ranks their simple average. Changes to the method will be logged on this page.

How the game scores you

You draft four films into five seats, so one seat stays empty. Each film scores its own number for the pillar it sits in. The empty seat scores your slate's lowest number in that pillar, so misreading a film costs you. Your score is the average of the five seats.

Blockbuster93 or higher, with no seat under 65
Smash Hit82 or higher, with no seat under 55
Solid Opening70 or higher
Underperformer52 or higher
FlopAnything below 52

A weak seat demotes you: a slate that clears 93 with a seat under 65 drops to the next tier it qualifies for. After each daily board closes, strong picks that few players made earn a rarity bonus. The obvious pick is rarely the best one.

Sources

IMDb non-commercial datasets (ratings, votes, credits) · Wikidata (box office, critic scores, CC0) · TMDB (posters, budget and revenue; this product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB) · OMDb (Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic) · DLu/oscar_data (Academy Award history) · Letterboxd public film pages, fetched once at build time (Letterboxd is a trademark of Letterboxd Limited; this site is unaffiliated) · BLS CPI-U for inflation.
